# Privacy Policy

**App:** Pagetalk 點讀書 · **Developer:** INFINITYPLAY (Infinity Playhouse Limited)

## The short version

Pagetalk does not collect your data, because it never receives it. The pages
you photograph, the words you save and your review progress are stored on your
iPhone or iPad only. We have no server, no account system and no way to read
anything you do in the app.

Pagetalk makes no network requests of its own: nothing is sent to us or to any
other company. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on the device

Everything you photograph, tap, save or record:

- the photos of book pages, menus, postcards or documents you add, and the
  text found in them
- your books, bookmarks and highlights
- your saved words and sentences, your streak and your review progress
- your settings (language, theme, voice and speed)

Delete a book, or delete the app, and the copy on that device is gone. If you
use iCloud Backup or an encrypted device backup, Apple may back up the app's
data as part of your device backup under your own Apple Account; that is
Apple's service, not ours.

## How the features work, and what they use

- **Reading the text in a picture** uses Apple's Vision framework, on the
  device. The picture and the text are not sent anywhere.
- **Reading aloud** uses Apple's speech voices on the device.
- **Translation** uses Apple's Translation framework. Translation runs on the
  device. The first time you use a language, iOS may ask you to download that
  language from Apple; that download is between your device and Apple.
- **Dictionary and example sentences** are built into the app and work offline.
  The "Detail" button opens the dictionary that comes with iOS.
- **Microphone and speech recognition** are used by Shadow reading (reading a
  sentence aloud to check it), **Listen** (live captions of a speaker) and
  **Talk** (face-to-face conversation). The app asks for permission only when
  you first use one of them, and it only ever uses **on-device** recognition
  (Apple's SpeechAnalyzer / Speech framework). The first time you use a
  language, iOS may download that language's speech model from Apple; that
  download is between your device and Apple. If a language cannot be
  recognised on the device, the feature stays off rather than sending your
  voice anywhere. Audio is processed as it arrives and is not stored or
  uploaded; only the text you choose to keep (for example a saved sentence)
  stays in the app.
- **Camera** is used when you scan a page and in **Camera translation**,
  which reads the text in front of the camera on the device and shows the
  translation over it. Camera frames are processed in memory and discarded;
  nothing is saved unless you tap "Save as book", and nothing is uploaded.
  Photos you pick from your library are copied into the app; the app asks for
  no permission to read your whole library.
- **Siri, Shortcuts and the Action button** can open Camera, Listen or Talk.
  They only open the app; no data is passed to them.

## What we do receive

Nothing you do in the app. One thing happens outside the app and is handled by
Apple, not by us:

- **Purchases.** Any paid features are sold through the App Store. Apple tells
  the app whether a purchase is active. We never see your name, your payment
  details or your Apple Account.

If you have agreed in iOS Settings to share analytics with developers, Apple
may give us aggregated crash and usage statistics. They contain none of your
books, words or photos.

## Analytics and advertising

There are none. No third-party SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no trackers.

## Children

Pagetalk is a general reading and language-learning tool. It collects no
personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete a book inside the app to remove its pictures and text, remove saved
words from the My Words screen, or delete the app to remove everything stored
on that device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
