# Support

**Pagetalk 點讀書**

Something wrong, or something missing? Open an issue:
<https://github.com/furmilyhk/tapread-site/issues>

## Common questions

**Does it need the internet?** No. Reading the text in a picture, the
dictionary, the voices and your word list all work offline. Translation uses
Apple's on-device translation; iOS may ask you to download a language once.

**Where is my data?** On your device only. Nothing is uploaded.

**It can't read my page.** Photograph it flat, in good light and close enough
that the text is sharp. Use the crop tool to straighten the page, and check
the book's language at the bottom right of the reading screen.

**The wrong language was chosen.** Tap the language at the bottom right of the
reading screen and pick the right one; the page is read again.

**Translation says it can't translate.** Open Settings ▸ Translation in iOS
and download the languages you need.

**Shadow reading is greyed out.** It only works for languages your device can
recognise offline. Nothing is sent to Apple's servers for it.

**I paid and the app still says free.** Open Settings inside the app and tap
**Restore purchases**, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel a subscription?** iOS Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions.
Deleting the app does not cancel it.
